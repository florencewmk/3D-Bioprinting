Queries are discovery attempts and can return no results, alternate domains or
outdated locations. They are not themselves evidence. Use the **actual source URLs
in `companies.csv`** for the supported facts, and retain the qualifications. For
example, TissueLabs' current source lists Bellinzona, not the initial Lugano search
term. Canadian Axolotl Biosciences and Turkish Axolotl Biosystems are different firms.

## Interpretation

- A dated company activity source is stronger historical evidence than a live product
  page with no date. Do not treat a webpage's crawl date or copyright footer as a 2025
  activity date.
- A 2026 announcement can support an explicitly dated historical fact, such as
  Allevi's 2023 production cessation. It is not silently treated as a 2025 source.
- A launch, product listing, customer installation, company filing and annual revenue
  are different observations. This register does not estimate annual sales.
- Supplier marketing statements about therapeutic outcomes, clinical superiority or
  regulatory status were not independently evaluated and are not used to rank firms.
- Generic scraped business directories returned many non-bioprinter businesses and
  were not used as a global manufacturer count.

## Remaining verification

This is a researched register rather than an exhaustive census. Twenty-one of the
40 records do not have an explicit 2025 activity flag; their actual historical status
is stated row by row. Additional work would seek dated 2025 catalogues, exhibitor
records, annual reports or customer installations, establish location changes, review
the remaining old-map candidates and broaden coverage in underrepresented markets.

Company cities have source-specific observation dates. Precise office coordinates,
branch inventories and a complete ultimate-parent ownership graph have not been
constructed. Do not turn the present sample into a definitive global company ranking.
