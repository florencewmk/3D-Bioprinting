# Custom map inputs

Prepared 3 November 2026 after the user authorized a custom interface with a black header and independent selection/expansion controls.

- `google-export-2026-11-03.kml`: unchanged download from the existing Google My Maps viewer's Export to KML control, Entire map, Export as KML. It contains 5,572 placemarks. Address-geocoded company/country points lack explicit coordinates in this export; the custom map does not pretend to have recovered them.
- `geonames-cities500.zip`: unchanged city gazetteer from https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities500.zip, downloaded 3 November 2026. GeoNames data is CC BY 4.0. The builder uses exact normalized city/alternate-name and country-code matches, taking the largest population when names repeat. Villaz-St-Pierre is explicitly normalized to Villaz-Saint-Pierre. The resulting 38 matches were reviewed, including Louisville KY, Victoria BC, Berkeley CA and Nanoscribe near Karlsruhe.
- `ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson`: unchanged Natural Earth public-domain country dataset from https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_50m_admin_0_countries.geojson. Country-summary markers use LABEL_X/LABEL_Y matched by ISO_A2_EH; this includes Singapore.
- `company-city-matches.json`: reviewed GeoNames match IDs, country/administrative codes and coordinates for all 38 company lineages.
- `provenance.json`: hashes, source URLs, categories and geometry definitions, generated by `tools/build_interactive_map.py`.

These extra geographic files do not update the scientific research window or imply that company evidence is from 2026. The parent dataset's 2025 evidence status and limitations remain visible in each marker. One institution remains unmapped. Research names, counts, evidence descriptions and institutional coordinates come from the archived My Maps inputs, unchanged.
