## Definitions and limitations

- Actual deceased donors are people from whom at least one organ was recovered for transplantation. This excludes living donors and registered potential donors.
- Transplant counts are organ-specific procedures, not unique recipients. For example, kidney–pancreas transplantation contributes to both organ categories.
- Waiting-list and death aggregates above are arithmetic sums of six organ categories (2023 report PDF p62; 2024 p65). They are not demonstrated deduplicated patient counts. Some patients can appear on multiple organ lists. Country coverage differs by organ, indicator, and year; the 2024 kidney table's 77 countries means countries providing any of the two indicators, not necessarily both.
- Do not interpret the 2023–2024 aggregate change as a controlled trend: reporting coverage changes.
- No comparable worldwide totals for waiting lists or waiting-list deaths were verified for 2015–2022 in this review. Earlier reports contain country charts, but missing values cannot be treated as zero or inferred from bar heights.
- Waiting-list deaths exclude deaths before registration, and can exclude deaths after removal from a list. They do not measure all deaths due to organ shortage, or establish that every death would have been prevented by transplantation.
- Year-end waiting counts are a snapshot; transplants and deaths are annual flows. Do not subtract them to calculate unmet need or an individual's probability of receiving an organ.
- 2016 transplant total (135,860) is the sum of the six organ totals on PDF p2. Its report explicitly includes older observations for countries missing 2016 data. Historical report totals may subsequently be revised: 2019's report reports 153,863 transplants, while 2020's retrospective comparison uses 157,301 for 2019. The February 2025 updated 2023 PDF reports 172,409, rather than 172,397 in the December 2024 PDF.
- 2024 report covers 92 member states, representing about 75% of world population. These figures are reported international activity, not complete enumeration of every country.
- 2024 living-donor activity is separately reported as 40,995 kidney, 9,865 liver, and 18 lung transplant procedures (50,878 in total; PDF p24). This is not a verified unique living-donor count; do not add it to deceased donors and label the result unique donors.
- The GODT data homepage also displays 2025 highlights (over 183,800 transplants; 49,169 deceased donors), alongside zero-valued counters and without a verified complete 2025 report here. These preliminary webpage figures are excluded from the report-based ten-year series.
